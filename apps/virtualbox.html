ABOUT THIS EMULATOR

This "VM" runs a real in-browser x86 emulator (v86) — it is not a fake animation. It boots small, BIOS-compatible ISOs directly from WebAssembly. Large modern operating system installers generally require hardware acceleration and BIOS/driver support this emulator doesn't provide, so they will fail or hang at boot. Good test ISOs: TinyCore Linux, FreeDOS, Damn Small Linux, Alpine Linux (netboot/standard).
